# Proposal

## Why

People with the published Windows beta.6 installer need to reach beta.16 from Settings. The previous public test stopped at beta.6. The direct beta.16 installation used a different path. [Issue #139](https://github.com/Ziyadin23/ScreenCompanion/issues/139)

## What Changes

Record what happens when a standard Windows 11 x64 account uses the packaged feed to download and install beta.16. Verify the installer bytes, the two exit paths, the installation identity, the saved settings and the restoration of the lab.

## Capabilities

This change qualifies existing behavior. It introduces no capability or specification changes.

## Impact

The public deliverables are the qualification record and its task checkpoint. The lab handoff and raw evidence stay in the private workspace.
